# Contributing

Thank you for improving DQ Speaker.

For a mechanical fit report, include:

- the STL or CAD revision you printed;
- printer, nozzle size, material and layer height;
- whether slicer XY compensation or model scaling was used;
- caliper measurements and clear photos of the mismatch;
- the relevant donor-module dimensions.

Keep changes focused. Do not commit proprietary LF03S firmware, vendor mobile
applications, marketplace screenshots, or files whose redistribution rights
are unclear.

For geometry changes, update the matching source script/specification, export
a fresh STEP/STL, and note whether the result was physically printed.
