# Assembly guide

## Hardware used by the current prototype

- 8 × M3 × 16 mm socket screws: four for the frame joint, four for the bottom
- 4 × M3 × 20 mm socket screws: top cap + optional V handle
- 12 × matching M3 heat-set inserts
- optional M2 × 10 mm screws/inserts for electronics mounting
- 4 × 10 mm rubber feet

The four top screw plugs are used only when the handle is omitted. They do not
install underneath the handle.

## Order of assembly

1. **Dry-fit the donor module.** Place it between the front and rear frame
   halves. Confirm the 4.2 mm front columns and 6 mm rear bosses align without
   forcing the donor speaker assembly.
2. **Install the frame inserts.** Heat-set them straight and allow the PETG to
   cool completely.
3. **Join the frame.** Use four M3 × 16 screws. Tighten gradually in a cross
   pattern and check that no screw, boss or rail crosses into the driver space.
4. **Install electronics.** Mount the LF03S board, power switch/encoder and
   battery. Add strain relief before routing wires toward the top openings.
5. **Fit the bottom cap.** Engage the frame and sleeve groove, then secure the
   bottom with four M3 × 16 screws.
6. **Slide on the sleeve.** It should key into the lower groove without glue.
   Do not force a warped sleeve; correct the print first.
7. **Fit the top cap.** Route USB-C and power hardware through the 11 mm and
   13 mm openings. The 8 mm internal service pocket provides tool clearance.
8. **Choose the top configuration.** Use the four small plugs for a handle-free
   top, or remove them and fit the centred V handle.
9. **Secure the top.** With the handle installed, use four M3 × 20 screws
   through handle → top cap → frame inserts. Do not use the available M3 × 30
   screws unless you first prove they cannot bottom out or strike hardware.
10. **Final test.** Check the switch, USB-C access, air path and any wire near a
    screw before powering the speaker.

The cap screws provide the clamping force that locks the grooved sleeve in
place. The sleeve should not be relied on as a structural battery mount.
